Department of Data Science

Vision

To create transformative impact on society through continual innovation in Data Science education, research, and applications — fostering creativity, entrepreneurship, and ethical data-driven development.

Mission

  • To deliver cutting-edge education in AI, ML, and Data Analytics blending theory and real-world practice.
  • To cultivate analytical and creative thinkers who solve complex problems using data insights.
  • To foster research and innovation through collaboration and entrepreneurship.
  • To empower students with global digital leadership and ethical data practices.
  • To build a community passionate about responsible and sustainable data science solutions.

Objectives

  • Deliver high-quality education in Data Science, AI, and related technologies to produce competent, industry-ready graduates.
  • Promote innovative research addressing challenges in business, healthcare, environment, and technology.
  • Encourage interdisciplinary collaboration across computing, statistics, and domain sciences.
  • Develop ethical, professional, and leadership qualities in students to thrive in a global data-driven world.
  • Strengthen industry engagement through consultancy, skill development, and collaborative projects for national growth.

About the Department

Leading the future of data-driven innovation

A Center of Excellence

The Department of Data Science at GM University is at the forefront of data analytics, machine learning, and artificial intelligence education. Established in 2018, our department has quickly become a center of excellence for data science education and research.

Where Theory Meets Practice

We offer comprehensive programs that blend theoretical foundations with practical applications, preparing students for careers in the rapidly evolving field of data science. Our curriculum is designed in collaboration with industry experts to ensure relevance and employability.

Message from Head of Department

Dear Students,

Welcome to the Department of Data Science at GM University, Davangere — a hub where knowledge meets innovation and data transforms into decisions. In today’s digital world, data is often called the “new oil.” At our department, we go beyond this idea — we believe data is the new intelligence that fuels discovery, research, and progress. We prepare our students to become innovators, problem-solvers, and visionaries in fields such as Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, and Cloud Computing.

Our teaching philosophy is simple: Learn, Apply, and Innovate. We blend rigorous academics with real-world applications through state-of-the-art laboratories, industry collaborations, hackathons, and research projects. Every student is encouraged to explore, experiment, and engineer solutions that can impact healthcare, finance, agriculture, business, and society at large.

We are not just building data professionals; we are nurturing data leaders who carry ethical values, creativity, and a spirit of lifelong learning. I invite you to join us on this exciting journey where curiosity meets creativity, and ideas evolve into innovations that can shape a smarter, more sustainable future.

Dr. Firoz Khan

Head of Department, Data Science

Our Programs

Cutting-edge curricula designed for the data-driven world

B.Tech in Data Science

A rigorous undergraduate program providing a deep dive into the mathematical, statistical, and computational foundations of Data Science. Students master tools for predictive modeling, data visualization, big data processing, and ethical data management.

  • Core focus on Python, R, SQL, and distributed computing (Spark).
  • Modules include Statistical Inference, Machine Learning, and Time Series Analysis.
  • Career paths: Data Scientist, Data Analyst, Business Intelligence Specialist.
